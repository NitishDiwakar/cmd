[PPP]

*Indian Contract Act, 1872*
  - Governs the legality, validity, performance obligations, and termination protocols of Concession Agreements signed between the government and a private enterprise.

*Specific Relief Act, 1963 (Section 20A)* 2018 amd.
  - prohibits civil courts from granting injunctions against scheduled infrastructure project contracts to prevent infrastructure project implementation delays.

*Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996*
  - Dictates the alternate dispute resolution, conciliation pathways, and international/domestic asset arbitrations

*Commercial Courts Act, 2015*
  - Mandates setting up fast-track, dedicated commercial divisions in High Courts for resolving high-value infrastructure or contract disputes.
