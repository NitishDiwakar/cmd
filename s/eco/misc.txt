[*Five Year Plans*]

*1. First Five-Year Plan (1951–1956)*
  - Harrod-Domar Model (focused heavily on high savings and capital accumulation)
  - Core Objective: Agriculture, irrigation, and price stabilization to recover from partition and WWII food shortages
  - Target: 2.1% | Achieved: 3.6%
  - Key milestones:
    -> Dams:  Bhakra-Nangal (Sutlej), Hirakud (Mahanadi), and Damodar Valley projects
    -> Five IITs were started; the University Grants Commission (UGC) was set up
    -> Community Development Programme (CDP): Launched on October 2, 1952

*2. Second Five-Year Plan (1956–1961)*
  - Mahalanobis Model (formulated by PC Mahalanobis, emphasizing rapid industrialization)
  - Core Objective: Rapid Industrialisation with a focus on heavy industries and the public sector (Socialistic pattern of society)
  - Target: 4.5% | Achieved: 4.27%
  - Milestones:
    -> 3 major steel plants
    -> Bhilai (Chhattisgarh) – Built with USSR assistance
    -> Rourkela (Odisha) – Built with Germany ast
    -> Durgapur (West Bengal) – Built with UK ast


*3. Third Five-Year Plan (1961–1966)*
  - Economic Model: Sukh Chakravarty and John Sandy Model (often called the Gadgil Yojana)
  - Core Objective: To make the economy independent and reach a self-generating/take-off stage. Focus shifted back to a balance between agriculture and heavy industry.
  - Target: 5.6% | Achieved: 2.4%
  - Milestones:
    -> Bokaro Steel Plant: Established in 1964 in Jharkhand (then Bihar) with USSR aid.
    -> Agricultural Price Commission (APC): Established in 1965 (later renamed CACP) to recommend MSP.
    -> Food Corporation of India (*FCI*): Set up in 1965


*The Plan Holiday (1966–1969)*


*4. Fourth Five-Year Plan (1969–1974)*
  - Gadgil Strategy (under Ashok Rudra and Alan Manne's open consistency framework).
  - Core Objective: "Growth with Stability" and progressive achievement of self-reliance.
  - Target: 5.7% | Achieved: 3.3% 
  - Milestones:
    -> Bank nationalisation: 14 banks, 19th July 1969
    -> Smiling Buddha: Pokharan, 1974
    -> ISRO: 1969
    -> MTRP (Monopolies and Restrictive Trade Practices) Act: 1969

*5. Fifth Five-Year Plan (1974–1978)*
  - Prepared and launched by D.D. Dhar
  - Core Objective: Garibi Hatao (Removal of poverty) and Attainment of Self-Reliance
  - Target: 4.4% | Achieved: 4.8%
  - The *Termination Trap*: This plan was terminated one year ahead of schedule in 1978 by the newly elected Morarji Desai (Janata Party) government, which launched the Rolling Plan (1978–1980) instead.
  - Milestones:
    -> Minimum Needs Programme (MNP): Introduced in 1974 to provide basic food, shelter, and health amenities
    -> Twenty Point Programme (TPP): Launched by Indira Gandhi in 1975
    -> Regional Rural Banks (RRBs): Established on October 2, 1975 (based on the Narasimham Committee recommendation)
