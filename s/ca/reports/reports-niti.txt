Macro and Fiscal Landscape of the State of Bihar (2025)
  - debt-to-GSDP ratio 39.6%
  - Central gvmt transfer ~75% of Bihar's total revenue receipts
  - *49.6%* of Bihar's workforce is engaged in *agriculture*


SDG India Index 2023-24 & 2025 Updates
  - BR bottom in major states
  - Scored: *52* out of 100 (*Performer* category, earlier: Aspirant)


Investment Friendliness Index (July 2026)
  - Highlights land availability (land banks) and high logistics costs for landlocked states like Bihar as major friction points. ( #mains )


Moving Towards Effective City Government: A Framework for Million-Plus Cities (April 2026)
  - Recommends improving the municipal revenue mechanism (Property Tax reforms) and standardizing spatial planning. ( #mains )


National Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI) - Progress Review
  - BR 52.4% down to approximately 34% (fastest declining)
  - Rural-urban disparities remain sharp, and nutrition/maternal health are highlighted as areas needing urgent policy shifts
