*Patna Metro Rail Project (Phase 1)*
  - Sanctioned Project Cost: ₹13,365.77 crore.
  - Funding Model: Jointly funded by the GOI (20%), Government of Bihar (20%), and a 60% loan from the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA)
  - Total length: 30.91 km
  - Corridor 1 (East-West): Danapur to Khemni Chak (17.78 km; 14 stations)
  - Corridor 2 (North-South): Patna Railway Station to New ISBT (14.20 km; 12 stations)
  - Track Gauge: Standard Gauge (1435 mm)


*Rajgir Sports Complex & Khel Academy*
  - Sanctioned Project Cost: Revised to ₹1,121.41 crore
  - Sports tourism


*Kachi Dargah – Bidupur Six-Lane Bridge*
  - Sanctioned Project Cost: Approx. ₹5,000 crore
  - Total Length: 9.76 km (main bridge length), stretching to 22.76 km including approach roads
  - Connectivity: Connects Kachi Dargah on NH-30 (Patna) to Bidupur on NH-103 (Vaishali)
  - Funding partner: ADB (Asian Development Bank)


*Danapur–Bihta Elevated Corridor*
  - ₹3,737 crore
  - 25 km long, four-lane elevated road network
