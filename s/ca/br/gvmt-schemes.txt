*Jannayak Karpoori Thakur Kisan Samman Nidhi*
  - Announced in the Bihar Budget 2026-27
  - ₹ 3000 Annually

Bihar Semiconductor Policy 2026
  - targets attracting ₹25,000 crore

Bal Hriday Yojana

Niji Talabon Ka Jirnoddhar Ki Yojana
  - Scheme focused on restoring private ponds to boost local irrigation, groundwater recharge, and pisciculture (fish farming)


[TECHNOLOGY, INDUSTRY & INVESTMENT]

Bihar AI Mission (2026)
  - Knowledge partner: IIT Patna
  - Industry partner: Tiger Analytics


Industrial Investment Promotion Policy (VIPPO - Modified amendment)
  - The *10-Acre Rule*: Any company investing over ₹100 crore and providing at least 1,000 direct local jobs receives 10 acres of land entirely free from BIADA


Bihar EV Policy Guidelines (Target 2030)
  - Goal: 30% of all newly registered vehicles must be electric by 2030.
  - Women's Subsidy: If an EV car is registered under a woman's name, a ₹1 Lakh direct cash subsidy is provided.
  - Two-Wheeler Subsidies: General category buyers receive ₹10,000; SC/ST category buyers receive ₹12,000.


[Emerging Agricultural Initiatives]

Strawberry Development Scheme
  - 40% financial subsidy

Samarth 2.0
  - Modernise textile skill ecosystem
  - focusing on strengthening Khadi, handlooms, and local handicrafts.


[Education]

Mission Daksh
  - For academically weaker students
  - 3:30 to 4:15 pm
  - 1:5 ratio (teacher to student)


[Cultural & Social Welfare Funds]

Mukhya Mantri Kalakar Pension Yojana
  - Eligibility Criteria: Artists engaged in classical, visual, or performing arts for a minimum of 10 years, aged 50 or above, with an annual family income below ₹1.2 Lakh.
  - Pension amount: ₹ 3,000 per month.


[Water]

Har Ghar Nal Ka Jal
  - Extended target: December 2028
